Prospectus Summary

We are a leading provider of cloud-based software solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. Our platform enables merchants to manage their operations across multiple sales channels.

Our business has grown rapidly, with revenue increasing from $50 million in 2020 to $200 million in 2022, representing a compound annual growth rate of 100%.

We define Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) as the subscription revenue we expect to receive in the following month, normalized to a monthly amount. MRR excludes one-time fees and variable usage charges.

Customer Acquisition Cost is calculated as total sales and marketing expenses divided by the number of new customers acquired in the period. We use this metric to assess the efficiency of our sales and marketing investments.

As of December 31, 2022, we had approximately 10,000 active merchant customers, up from 3,000 at the end of 2020. We define an active merchant as one who processed at least one transaction in the last 30 days.

Risk Factors

Our business is subject to various risks including competition from larger established players, changes in technology, and economic downturns that may reduce merchant spending on software solutions.

We have a limited operating history as a public company and may face challenges in maintaining our growth rate as we scale our operations and expand into new markets.
